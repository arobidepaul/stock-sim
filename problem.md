## Problem

People who are interested in stock trading have no safe way to practice without risking real money. Beginners don't understand how market changes affect a portfolio, and there's no simple way to experiment without buying and selling.

## Initial requirements

- R1: The player starts with a set amount of fake money
- R2: The game has at least 3 fake companies with stock prices
- R3: Stock prices change each turn based on random events
- R4: The player can buy and sell stocks each turn
- R5: The game ends after a set number of turns
- R6: The player's final portfolio value is their score
- R7: The game displays a graph of each stock's price history
